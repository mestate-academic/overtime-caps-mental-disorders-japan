# Data dictionary

All files are UTF-8 CSV with a header row. Category labels are kept in Japanese exactly as published by the source so that they can be checked against the originals; English equivalents are given below. `fy` is the Japanese fiscal year (April–March; FY2025 = April 2025–March 2026). Counts are numbers of cases unless stated otherwise. The files are generated by `code/build_data*.py`, in which each block of values carries a comment naming the source table; the committed CSVs are identical to the scripts' output.

## Files used in the paper

### `rosai_mental_by_industry.csv` — mental disorders, workers' compensation by industry
Source: MHLW, 過労死等の労災補償状況, annex table 2-2 (精神障害の業種別請求・決定・支給決定件数), FY2011–FY2025 editions; each edition lists the current and previous fiscal year, and overlapping years were cross-checked. Approvals were also checked against MHLW Labour Standards Bureau, 精神障害の労災補償状況 (検討会資料2, 2021).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `fy` | fiscal year, 2011–2025 (claims available from FY2016) |
| `industry` | industry division (日本標準産業分類 大分類): 建設業 construction; 運輸業郵便業 transport and postal services; 製造業 manufacturing; 卸売小売業 wholesale and retail; 金融保険業 finance and insurance; 教育学習支援業 education; 情報通信業 information and communication; 宿泊飲食 accommodation and food services; 医療福祉 health care and welfare; 農林漁鉱 agriculture, forestry, fisheries and mining; その他(サービス業含む) other (residual); 合計 total |
| `approvals` | 支給決定件数 — claims approved as work-related in that fiscal year |
| `claims` | 請求件数 — claims filed in that fiscal year |

### `rosai_cvd_by_industry.csv` — cerebro-cardiovascular disease by industry (positive control)
Source: same reports, annex table 1-2 (脳・心臓疾患の業種別). Columns as above: `fy` (2016–2025), `industry`, `cvd_claims`, `cvd_approvals`.

### `rosai_mental_national.csv` — mental disorders, national totals
Source: same reports, annex table 2-1 (精神障害の請求・決定件数). `fy` (2002–2025), `claims`, `decisions` (決定件数, claims decided), `approvals`.

### `rosai_mental_by_event.csv` — approvals by precipitating event, FY2020–2025
Source: same reports, annex table 2-8 (出来事別決定及び支給決定件数); FY2020 from 検討会資料2 (2021). `fy`, `event`, `decisions`, `approvals`. Events (labels follow the recognition criteria revised 29 May 2020 and 1 September 2023): パワハラ（上司等） power harassment by supervisors; 同僚等からのいじめ・暴行 bullying or violence by co-workers; セクハラ sexual harassment; カスハラ（2023.9新設） customer harassment (listed from September 2023); 仕事内容・仕事量の大きな変化 major change in work content or workload; 月80時間以上の時間外労働 ≥80 hours of overtime in a month; 2週間以上の連続勤務 ≥2 weeks of continuous work; 悲惨な事故・災害の体験 experience of a tragic accident or disaster; 重度の病気やケガ severe illness or injury; 特別な出来事（極度の負荷等） special events (extreme load); 合計 total. FY2016–2019 (under the 2011 criteria, with different categories) are transcribed inside `code/build_events_2016.py`, which produces the ten-year table (`output/intermediate/t24_events_2016_2025.csv`, Table 3 of the paper).

### `rosai_mental_by_pref.csv` — mental disorders by prefecture, FY2021–2025
Source: same reports, annex table 2-5 (都道府県別). `fy`, `pref` (prefecture name in Japanese), `claims`, `decisions`, `approvals`.

### `employment_by_industry.csv` — employed persons by industry (denominators)
Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, Labour Force Survey, basic tabulation, table 2-5-1, annual averages, in 10 000 persons. `year` (calendar year, 2009–2025; used as the denominator of the fiscal year beginning in that year) and one column per industry division (Japanese labels as in the survey; 全産業 all industries). Sub-divisions (道路旅客運送業, 道路貨物運送業, 医療業, 社会保険福祉介護) are included for reference only.

### `census2015_pref_industry.csv` — 2015 Census employment by prefecture (exposure intensity)
Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, 2015 Population Census, employment status basic tabulation, table 00510. `pref`, `emp_total`, `emp_const` (construction), `emp_transport` (transport and postal), `emp_medwel` (health care and welfare), `emp_manuf` (manufacturing); persons. Exposure in the prefecture panel = (`emp_const`+`emp_transport`)/`emp_total`, standardised.
